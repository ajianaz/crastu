Crastu

A personal voice and video studio for macOS, then Windows. Dictation, text to speech, an auto-clipper and a timeline editor in one local app that AI agents can drive over MCP.

Five principles

  1. A tool, not a showcase. The user's media and words stand out. The chrome stays quiet and consistent.
  2. One signal colour. The accent marks the primary action, the playhead and the current selection. Red is reserved for recording.
  3. Time is data. Timecodes and durations use tabular monospace digits, always exact.
  4. AI is visible and steerable. Every AI suggestion shows a score, a reason, and accept or reject. Actions from external agents follow the approval policy and land in one log.
  5. Feels native on a Mac. Keyboard first, ⌘ shortcuts, visible focus, calm motion that can be turned off.
